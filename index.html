02 / Effect

Prism

Mono in. Space out.

Give mono synths and arpeggios a stereo field with width, diffusion and gentle movement.

  • Stereo synthesis with Bass Focus
  • Width and near/far depth shaping
  • Live stereoscope and mono audition

macOS, Windows & Linux · AU / VST3*

03 / Effect

Delayed

Lock the groove. Build the tension.

A compact, tempo-synced techno delay for rhythmic echoes, ping-pong movement and the bars before a drop.

  • Host-synced rhythms with dotted and triplet options
  • Ping-pong echoes, feedback tone and ducking
  • Automate BUILD for intensity; hit DROP to clear the echoes

macOS, Windows & Linux · AU / VST3*

In development · Release details to come
